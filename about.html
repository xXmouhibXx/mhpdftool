About M.HPDFTOOL

Small, free tools for everyday paperwork.

What this site is

M.HPDFTOOL is a collection of free tools, in English, French and Arabic, for the small tasks that come up when you deal with documents: shrinking a PDF so an online form accepts it, turning a scanned page into editable text, writing an amount in words on a cheque, or converting between PDF and images.

Who runs it

The site is built and maintained by an independent developer in Tunisia. The amount-in-words tools are made for the Tunisian dinar and its millimes, in French and Arabic.

How the tools work

Every tool runs inside your browser. Your files and the amounts you type are not uploaded to a server.

How the site is funded

The tools are free. The site may show advertising to cover its costs.
